Kill a cat, and you could be executed
So they wrapped it in linen
and buried it like kings.
Cats were never decoration.
The grain drew rats.
The cats ended the rats.
When a cat died, the household shaved its eyebrows
Every last one
if Herodotus was telling the truth.
Then devotion became an industry.
Pilgrims bought cat mummies at Bubastis
as gifts for Bastet — priests supplied the demand.
Hundreds of thousands were sold.
Most were not pets but offerings
letters to a goddess — bronze cats for the poor.
The cat was never just loved.
Worshipped, farmed, and sold.
So — what happened if you killed one?
